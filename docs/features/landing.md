# Landing

**Status:** building
**Type:** user-facing feature

## Purpose
Single-page marketing site that explains what OmniGrowth is and converts visitors into signups. Hero CTA navigates to login / signup.

## In scope
- Hero with one-liner + primary CTA
- Pillar / capability sections
- Visual showcase (AI-assisted post creation, video pipeline, calendar, analytics)
- Footer with legal links
- Single-page only (no multi-route marketing site for v1)

## Out of scope
- Blog
- Standalone pricing page (handled inside the app under `settings-billing` for now)
- Documentation portal
- Multi-language

## Depends on
- Brand visual identity (final design coming separately)
- `auth` feature (CTA navigates to login)

## Used by
- New visitors (top of funnel)

## Open questions
- Final visual design (waiting on user-provided design)
- Pricing on the landing or only inside the app?
